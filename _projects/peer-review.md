This project applies peer prediction to academic reviewing, using reviewers' predictions of others' responses to encourage more honest and reliable feedback. I developed a HotCRP question type that lets reviewers estimate peer scores with a slider, providing additional signals for adjusting review assessments.

[View the code](https://github.com/ziyin-xiong/hotcrp).
