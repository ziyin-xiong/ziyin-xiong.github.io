At the University of California, Berkeley, I explored symmetry regularization for high-speed quadruped locomotion. Inspired by diagonal limb symmetry and time-reversal symmetry in animal motion, the project improved tracking velocity and stability in Legged Gym simulations of the Unitree Go1.

[View the code](https://github.com/ziyin-xiong/Go1-Locomotion).
